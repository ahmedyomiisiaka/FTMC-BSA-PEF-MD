## 11 September 2026

### Objective
Begin preparation for computational analysis of bovine serum
albumin (BSA) and its possible response to electric-field/PEF
conditions.

### Literature review
Reviewed literature concerning electric-field effects on proteins,
with particular attention to BSA. Experimental evidence suggests
that moderate electric-field exposure can produce partial structural
changes, while stronger exposure can cause more extensive
denaturation or aggregation.

## 12–13 September 2026 — BSA Control MD System Preparation and Equilibration

### Objective

Prepare and equilibrate a baseline all-atom BSA system before any
electric-field simulation. The control system is intended to provide
a reference against which later electric-field simulations can be
compared.

No external electric field was applied during the work documented
in this section.

### Final starting structure

PDB 4F5S chain A was selected as the working BSA monomer.

The processed structure contains 583 residues corresponding to the
mature BSA chain.

Sequence comparison against the mature UniProt P02769 sequence
identified one documented difference:

- PDB mature-chain residue 190: THR
- Corresponding UniProt canonical residue 214: ALA

The PDB file documents this position as a sequence variant. A
canonical working structure was therefore generated by restoring
THR190 to ALA190 while preserving the original downloaded structure
separately.

After this correction:

- UniProt mature BSA length: 583 residues
- Processed PDB chain A length: 583 residues
- Sequence identity: 100.00%

### Disulfide-bond validation

The BSA monomer contains 35 cysteine residues.

Seventeen disulfide bonds were verified:

53–62, 75–91, 90–101, 123–168, 167–176, 199–245,
244–252, 264–278, 277–288, 315–360, 359–368,
391–437, 436–447, 460–476, 475–486, 513–558,
and 557–566.

Cys34 remains the free cysteine in mature-chain PDB numbering.

### Force field and water model

GROMACS 2023.3 (Ubuntu package) was used for the MD preparation.

The following baseline models were selected:

- Protein force field: AMBER99SB-ILDN
- Water model: TIP3P

These were selected as established all-atom models available in the
installed GROMACS environment. They are recorded as the models used
for this project rather than being considered universally optimal.

### Topology generation

The canonical BSA structure was processed with `gmx pdb2gmx`.

The generated protein topology contained:

- 583 protein residues
- 9216 protein atoms
- Approximate protein mass: 66383.011 amu
- Initial protein charge: -16 e

The topology contained 34 CYX residues, corresponding to the 17
expected disulfide bonds. The remaining free cysteine was retained
as CYS.

### Simulation box

A dodecahedral periodic box was constructed using `gmx editconf`.

A minimum distance of 1.0 nm was placed between the protein and the
box boundary.

The resulting box volume before equilibration was approximately
1027.94 nm^3.

### Solvation

The system was solvated with three-point water coordinates using
`gmx solvate`.

The topology remained configured for TIP3P water.

Initial number of water molecules:

29,950

The solvated system initially contained approximately 99,066 atoms.

### Ions and ionic strength

The protein carried a net charge of -16 e before ion addition.

The system was neutralized and prepared at a nominal NaCl
concentration of 0.15 M using `gmx genion`.

Ions added:

- Na+: 109
- Cl-: 93

The difference of 16 positive ions neutralized the -16 e protein
charge.

202 water molecules were replaced during ion placement.

Final composition after ion addition:

- Protein molecules: 1
- Water molecules: 29,748
- Na+ ions: 109
- Cl- ions: 93
- Net system charge: 0

The 0.15 M NaCl condition is used as a baseline simulation condition
and is not assumed to reproduce the ionic composition of the
experimental PEF system unless this is established separately.

### Energy minimization

Energy minimization was performed using the steepest-descent
algorithm.

Main settings:

- Maximum steps: 50,000
- emtol: 1000 kJ mol^-1 nm^-1
- emstep: 0.01 nm
- Electrostatics: PME
- Periodic boundary conditions: xyz

The minimization converged after 1,206 steps.

Final minimization results:

- Potential energy: -1.5524191e+06 kJ/mol
- Maximum force: 9.9985583e+02 kJ mol^-1 nm^-1
- Norm of force: 1.6501566e+01 kJ mol^-1 nm^-1

The convergence criterion Fmax < 1000 kJ mol^-1 nm^-1 was satisfied.

### NVT equilibration

A 100 ps NVT equilibration was performed with protein position
restraints.

Main conditions:

- Temperature: 300 K
- Duration: 100 ps
- Time step: 2 fs
- Thermostat: V-rescale
- Temperature coupling groups: Protein and non-Protein
- Pressure coupling: none
- Hydrogen-containing bonds constrained with LINCS

The simulation completed all 50,000 steps successfully.

Temperature statistics over 0–100 ps:

- Average temperature: 299.827 K
- RMSD: 2.927 K
- Total drift: 1.210 K

The NVT temperature was therefore considered adequately maintained
around the 300 K target.

### NPT equilibration

A 100 ps NPT equilibration was subsequently performed with protein
position restraints.

Main conditions:

- Temperature: 300 K
- Target pressure: 1 bar
- Duration: 100 ps
- Time step: 2 fs
- Thermostat: V-rescale
- Barostat: C-rescale
- Pressure coupling: isotropic
- tau_p: 5.0 ps
- Compressibility: 4.5e-5 bar^-1
- `refcoord_scaling = com`

The simulation completed all 50,000 steps successfully.

Temperature over 0–100 ps:

- Average: 300.024 K
- RMSD: 1.013 K
- Total drift: -0.424 K

Pressure over 0–100 ps:

- Average: -21.33 bar
- RMSD: 133.67 bar
- Error estimate: 32 bar

Because pressure fluctuates strongly in finite MD systems, the
second half of the trajectory was also examined.

Pressure over 50–100 ps:

- Average: 3.60 bar
- Error estimate: 6.1 bar
- RMSD: 99.96 bar
- Total drift: -10.86 bar

Density over 0–100 ps:

- Average: 1011.58 kg/m^3
- RMSD: 5.56 kg/m^3
- Total drift: 7.57 kg/m^3

Density over 50–100 ps:

- Average: 1012.71 kg/m^3
- Error estimate: 0.43 kg/m^3
- RMSD: 1.41 kg/m^3
- Total drift: -1.28 kg/m^3

The substantially smaller density fluctuation and drift during the

The 100 ps NPT stage was therefore accepted as the initial

## 26 September 2026 — Microsecond PEF-to-MD Translation and Pilot Planning

### Objective

Define a scientifically defensible strategy for investigating the
microsecond PEF experiments computationally before starting production
electric-field MD.

The microsecond regime is being considered first, following the
internship research direction. Nanosecond PEF will be investigated
later using the same BSA model and core analysis framework so that the
two experimental regimes can eventually be compared.

### Experimental anchor

The primary microsecond experimental reference is Taha et al. (2022),
which investigated BSA/starch under PEF conditions including:

- Pulse duration: 50 μs
- Number of pulses: 10
- Electric-field strengths: 3.5, 4.5, 5.7 and 8.1 kV/cm

These correspond to:

- 3.5 kV/cm = 0.00035 V/nm
- 4.5 kV/cm = 0.00045 V/nm
- 5.7 kV/cm = 0.00057 V/nm
- 8.1 kV/cm = 0.00081 V/nm

The cumulative nominal field-on time for 10 × 50 μs pulses is
500 μs.

### Important timescale distinction

The experimental pulse duration and the MD trajectory duration are
different quantities.

For example:

- 50 μs experimental pulse = 50,000 ns
- 30 ns MD trajectory = 0.03 μs

Therefore, an MD trajectory lasting tens of nanoseconds must not be
described as a direct simulation of a 50 μs experimental pulse.

The current computational work is instead described as an MD
investigation motivated by the experimental microsecond-PEF study.

### Experimental-to-MD interpretation

The initial BSA-only simulations will not directly reproduce the full
BSA/starch experiment.

The following relationships will be used cautiously:

- DLS particle size → Rg may provide information about molecular
  compactness, but Rg is not equivalent to DLS particle size.
- Surface hydrophobicity → hydrophobic SASA may provide an indirect
  molecular interpretation.
- Intrinsic fluorescence → local aromatic-residue environment,
  contacts and solvent exposure may provide indirect interpretation.
- Grafting/glycation → not directly represented in the initial
  BSA-only classical MD system.
- Solubility and emulsifying properties → macroscopic properties
  without direct single-protein MD equivalents.

Additional MD analyses will include RMSD, RMSF, intramolecular
hydrogen bonds, secondary structure and protein dipole response.

### Pilot simulation concept

A small pilot is planned before any larger production simulation
campaign.

The current concept contains:

1. A no-field BSA control.
2. An experimental-scale electric-field condition anchored to the
   2022 microsecond study.
3. A literature-supported mechanistic electric-field condition that
   can help determine whether BSA shows detectable molecular response
   on an accessible atomistic-MD timescale.

The stronger mechanistic condition, if used, will not be described as
a numerical or temporal equivalent of the experimental 50 μs pulse.

Candidate values discussed during planning include 0.00057 V/nm as an
experimental-field anchor and 0.0012 V/nm as a BSA-specific
literature-supported comparison condition.

These remain candidate pilot parameters and are not yet finalized
production conditions.

### Field direction and electrical response

The external electric field is a vector. BSA orientation and its
molecular dipole may therefore influence the observed response.

The pilot analysis should distinguish between:

- orientational/electrostatic response to the field; and
- persistent structural changes in the protein.

Dipole magnitude/components and, where appropriate, the angle between
the protein dipole and the applied field will therefore be considered
alongside structural metrics.

### Computing considerations

The current local GROMACS installation is CPU-only and previously
achieved approximately 1.1 ns/day for the solvated BSA system.

Longer production simulations and replicate simulations would
therefore benefit substantially from GPU/server resources.

FTMC GPU access is currently being investigated. In the meantime,
protocol preparation, documentation, short validation tests and
analysis planning can continue locally.

### Current decision point

No long production electric-field simulation will be launched yet.

The next computational step is to inspect the existing control
production configuration, validate the control workflow, verify the
electric-field implementation for GROMACS 2023.3, and then prepare a
small microsecond-experiment-inspired pilot.

The exact production trajectory duration, electric-field conditions,
pulse representation and replicate strategy will be finalized only
after the pilot design and computational resources have been checked.
